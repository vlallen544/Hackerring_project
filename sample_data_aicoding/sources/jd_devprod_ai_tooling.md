# Job Description: Developer Productivity Engineer – AI Tooling, Chennai (Campus Hiring 2026)

## Responsibilities
Roll out AI coding assistants and coding agents to 400 engineers safely.
Show leadership whether the tools really make teams faster and safer.

## Required skills
Building CI guardrails for AI-assisted changes: tests, linters, secret scanning and dependency checks.
Running coding agents with least privilege, away from production secrets.
Measuring developer productivity with delivery metrics such as lead time and change failure rate.
Writing team guidelines for responsible use of AI coding tools.

## Good to have
Experience with GitHub Actions or another CI system.
