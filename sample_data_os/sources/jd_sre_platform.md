# Job Description: Site Reliability Engineer – Platform Team, Pune (Campus Hiring 2026)

## Responsibilities
Keep production Linux systems reliable and respond to incidents.
Automate the deployment and monitoring of services.

## Required skills
Managing Linux services with systemd, which runs as PID 1 on our servers.
Diagnosing memory pressure, page faults and swapping on busy machines.
Understanding of process states, signals and CPU scheduling.
Debugging race conditions and deadlocks in multithreaded services.

## Good to have
Scripting in Bash or Python and experience with Prometheus or Grafana.
