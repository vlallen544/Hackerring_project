Viva_

A short spoken interview. Answer in your own words. Vague answers get a follow-up question.

  2. Type your answer, or press Speak and say it.
  3. Tell it how sure you were (1-5). It spots over- and under-confidence.
Follow-up question

How sure are you? (1 = guessing, 5 = certain)
